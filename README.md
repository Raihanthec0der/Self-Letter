# 👻💌 Dear Self — A Letter to My Future Self

> **A small letter from who I am today to who I will become tomorrow.**

**Dear Self** is a personal motivational project created to preserve my journey, struggles, dreams, lessons, and future goals.

This project is more than just a letter. It is a promise to myself:

**Keep learning. Keep building. Keep improving. Never forget where you started.**

> *“Every effort you make today is shaping the person you will become tomorrow.”*

## 🌱 Purpose

This project exists as a personal reminder to:

* 📚 Keep learning and growing
* 💪 Stay consistent when things get difficult
* 🧠 Remember the struggles and lessons of the past
* 🌟 Appreciate how far I've already come
* 🚀 Believe in my future self
* 🌱 Turn small daily efforts into meaningful progress
* ❤️ Make my past self proud
